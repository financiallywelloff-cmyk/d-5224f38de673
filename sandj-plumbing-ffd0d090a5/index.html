How it runs (sample)

Your Friends & Family Club promises pre-scheduled visits: water heater flush in fall, sump pump inspection in spring, sewer video inspection in summer. Each season, the assistant pulls every member who is due and drafts a reminder.

Reminder text (draft)

Hi [first name], it's S and J Plumbing & Sewer. Your Friends & Family Club water heater flush is due this fall. Reply with a day that works (weekday or Saturday) and we'll lock in a time.

What happens to replies

  • “Tuesday morning”: proposed slot sent to dispatch to confirm
  • “Moved / cancel”: flagged for the membership line, since cancellations need to be in writing
  • No reply after 2 texts: added to a CSR call list
