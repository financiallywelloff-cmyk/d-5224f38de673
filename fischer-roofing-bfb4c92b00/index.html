1

Messy job note → proposal draft + crew sheet

An estimator's walk-through notes become a customer-ready proposal draft and a one-glance crew sheet in one step, with open questions flagged instead of guessed.

From your job post:
  • “Set up AI tools for drafting proposals… summarizing job notes”
  • “estimates, scheduling, ordering”

Before (what the estimator typed in the truck)

Site-visit note (sample)
walked 14 [sample] ct jackson w/ HO today. 2 story colonial approx 28 sq. 1 layer arch, granules gone on south side. couple soft spots near rear chimney prob 4-5 sheets ply. chimney flashing shot - needs step + counterflash. HO wants GAF timberline HDZ charcoal. ice+water eaves & valleys. swap the 3 box vents for ridge vent. gutters ok leave them. DOG in yard!! wants done before thanksgiving. driveway steep - dumpster on street? check town permit

After (AI draft, for the estimator to check)

Proposal draft

Roof Replacement Proposal (DRAFT)

Prepared for: [Homeowner] · [Address], Jackson, NJ

Scope of work

  • Remove one (1) layer of architectural shingles to the deck (approx. 28 squares); haul away all debris.
  • Inspect decking after tear-off; replace damaged plywood as found. Estimated 4–5 sheets near rear chimney, billed per sheet at [unit price].
  • Ice & water shield at all eaves and valleys; [underlayment product] on the remaining field.
  • Install GAF Timberline HDZ shingles, color Charcoal, with matching hip & ridge.
  • Remove three (3) box vents, deck over openings, install continuous ridge vent.
  • New step and counter flashing at rear chimney.
  • Existing gutters to remain and be protected during work.
  • Daily cleanup and magnetic nail sweep.

Price: [from your estimating sheet] · Warranty: [per your GAF certification]

Timing: Customer asked for completion before Thanksgiving (Nov 26). [confirm crew slot]

Flagged for the estimator
• Steep driveway: dumpster on the street may need a Jackson Township permit.
• Deck count is an estimate until tear-off, so keep the per-sheet line.
• Underlayment brand not specified in note.
Crew sheet
  • 2-story colonial · ~28 sq · 1-layer tear-off
  • GAF Timberline HDZ, Charcoal + hip & ridge
  • Load: ice & water (eaves + valleys), ridge vent, ~6 sheets ply, chimney step/counter flashing
  • Watch: soft deck at rear chimney
  • DOG on property: confirm it's inside before setup
  • Dumpster on street (permit?) · protect gutters

How it would run for you: The estimator sends a note or voice memo from the truck (text, email, or the notes field in your CRM). The draft lands in the estimating queue with prices left as blanks for your team to fill. AI never invents a price.

2

Every inbound lead routed to the right division, with a reply sent fast

Voicemails, web forms and emails get sorted to Commercial, Sloped, Siding, Service, Maintenance or Designer Panels, with urgency, missing info, and a drafted first reply. Public reviews praise your fast emergency response and office scheduling; a few mention slow estimate follow-up. This makes the fast experience the default.

From your job post:
  • “answering customer questions”
  • “customer follow-up”
  • “connect our CRM, estimating… and scheduling tools”

Before (three things that hit the office on a Monday)

Voicemail transcript (sample)
hi uh calling from [sample] property management, we have like four buildings in Lakewood, one has water coming into the top floor hallway since the storm last night, flat roof I think, can someone call me back today, 732-555-0142
Website form (sample)
looking for a price on new siding, its vinyl from the 90s, maybe the roof soon too? Toms River. no rush, spring is fine

Sol, 12 townhomes in Phase 2 ready for roofing mid-November, same spec as Phase 1. Also the architect wants to talk ACM panels on the clubhouse facade. Can you send numbers by Friday?

Instant text to the property manager (draft)
Got your message about the leak at your Lakewood building. A Fischer service manager will call you within the hour. If you can, reply with the building address and how we get roof access.

How it would run for you: Your phone system's voicemail-to-text, the website form and the office inbox feed one triage step. The office coordinator sees a sorted list and approves replies with one tap. Nothing goes to a customer without a person saying yes until you trust it.

3

Review replies + post-job check-ins that catch problems early

Drafts owner-quality replies to new reviews, turns a complaint into a service ticket with a due date, and texts customers a week after the job, before a small issue becomes a 1-star review.

From your job post:
  • “customer service”
  • “customer follow-up”
  • “processing paperwork”

Before (two new reviews, nobody's had time)

New 5★ review (sample)
Storm was an hour out and they sent a crew same day to dry in our addition. Kept our job on schedule, super professional, cleaned everything up.
New 2★ review (sample)
They redid siding by my window to fix a leak. Still leaking and nobody called me back after I emailed twice.

After (drafted for a manager to approve)

Reply to the 5★ (draft)
Thank you! Glad the crew got your addition dried in before the storm hit. We'll pass this along to the team. — Fischer Roofing
Reply to the 2★ (draft)
We're sorry the leak is still there, and sorry you didn't hear back. That's not how we want to treat anyone. Our service manager will call you by [date] to set up a return visit and find the source. — Fischer Roofing
Auto-created service ticket
  • Callback owed: open leak after siding repair, window area
  • Customer emailed twice, no response logged
  • Assign: Service Division · Due: within 24 hours
  • Link to review + original job record
7-day check-in text (template)
Hi [first name], it's Fischer Roofing. It's been a week since we finished your job. Anything look or sound off after the rain? Reply here and we'll take care of it.

How it would run for you: New reviews and finished jobs trigger the drafts. A manager approves each public reply. The check-in only asks for a review if the customer's reply is happy, and anything else becomes a ticket instead.
