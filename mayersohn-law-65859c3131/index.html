What the routing adds (in GoHighLevel)

  • Prenup: Family Law → book a strategy session; tag the June date
  • Closing Friday: Real Estate → same-day callback; ask for the contract + addendum
  • Lease: Commercial Leasing & Retail → request the draft lease, book a review call
